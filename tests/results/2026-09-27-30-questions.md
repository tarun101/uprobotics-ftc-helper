The Competition Integrity Contract (CIC) is a set of guidelines that outlines the expected behavior of teams in FIRST Tech Challenge. It is divided into two categories: Sporting Ethics Code and Behavior Guidelines. The CIC defines ways that every team should behave, both at and outside events, to protect the integrity of the competition and embody Gracious Professionalism.

According to the [Sporting Ethics Code](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20TU02.htm#Sporting_Ethics_Code), teams certify that they don't cheat, always behave with integrity, play the game as intended, and know that their team doesn't enforce the rules.

The CIC is described in [Section 1.5 Competition Integrity Contract (CIC)](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20TU02.htm#_Toc235883760) of the Competition Manual.
