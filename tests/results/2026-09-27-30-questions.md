The Competition Integrity Contract (CIC) is a set of guidelines that outlines the expected behavior of teams in FIRST Tech Challenge. It is divided into two categories: the Sporting Ethics Code and Behavior Guidelines. The CIC defines ways that every team should behave, both at and outside events, to protect the integrity of the competition and embody Gracious Professionalism.

According to [Section 1.5 Competition Integrity Contract (CIC)](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20TU02.htm#_Toc235883760), the CIC includes a Sporting Ethics Code, which states that teams should not cheat, behave with integrity, and play the game as intended.

[Rule G202 *Follow the Competition Integrity Contract (CIC)](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20TU02.htm#G202) requires teams to follow the CIC during every match. 

More information about the CIC can be found in [Section 1.5.1 Sporting Ethics Code](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20TU02.htm#Sporting_Ethics_Code) and [Section 1.5.3 Infractions, Mitigations, & Escalation](https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20TU02.htm#Infractions).
