The Competition Integrity Contract (CIC) is a set of guidelines that defines ways every team in FIRST Tech Challenge should behave, both at and outside events. It includes a Sporting Ethics Code and Behavior Guidelines that outline team behaviors to protect the integrity of the competition and ensure fair play.

The CIC is divided into two categories: 
1. Sporting Ethics Code, which all teams must follow to protect the integrity of the competition, 
2. Behavior Guidelines, which help teams act in a way that embodies Gracious Professionalism and ensures every team can have fun, make friends, and chase excellence.
