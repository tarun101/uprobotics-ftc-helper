## 22. [Game] What are POLLEN and NECTAR and how do you score them?

**Cited:** manual--sec-8--e8330e03.md, manual--sec-1.3.4--22b13173.md, manual--sec-9.8--e3f63fa7.md, manual--G418--9d722089.md, manual--sec-1.7.4--3fcd3945.md, manual--G427--bbbe196d.md, manual--G411--cb606383.md, manual--sec-10.5.3--bb609966.md, manual--sec-10.5.2--0e8472bc.md, manual--sec-10.1--cbff8efc.md

POLLEN and NECTAR are the SCORING ELEMENTS in the BIOBUZZ game. POLLEN are approximately 2.8 in. (7.1 cm) yellow plastic balls, and NECTAR are approximately 3.6 in. (9.1 cm) red and blue plastic balls. 

To score POLLEN and NECTAR, ALLIANCES collect and place them into their HIVE, GARDEN, or FLOWERS. For HIVE scoring, when enough SCORING ELEMENTS are LAUNCHED into the CELL, the HIVE will tip, earning points. For GARDEN scoring, POLLEN or NECTAR must be at least partially in the GARDEN zone. For FLOWER scoring, NECTAR and POLLEN score when they are at least partially within the FLOWER scoring volume. The ALLIANCE that has the top-most NECTAR of its color in a FLOWER owns that FLOWER and earns points for every POLLEN and NECTAR that meet the scoring criteria for that FLOWER.

Rules: Section 9.8 SCORING ELEMENTS, Section 10.5.2 FLOWER Scoring Criteria, Section 10.5.3 GARDEN Scoring Criteria. 
Link: https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20V1.htm#_Toc239421276, https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20V1.htm#_Ref237946722, https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/cm-html/BIOBUZZ%20Competition%20Manual%20-%20V1.htm#_Toc209089384
